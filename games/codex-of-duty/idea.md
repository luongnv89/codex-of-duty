# Idea — Codex of Duty

## Design brief

Upgrade Codex of Duty into an AAA-style 3D first-person browser shooter built around moving through a hostile city, activating relay objectives, and surviving tactical enemy encounters. The moment-to-moment loop blends traversal, cover, weapon switching, precise shooting, resource pickups, and visible mission progression across multiple escalating stages. It uses a first-person camera with keyboard-and-mouse controls plus complete touch equivalents. The presentation should pursue exceptional browser-game quality: readable silhouettes, dramatic lighting, responsive HUD feedback, strong onboarding, clear menus, and layered generated sound. Its signature is a relay-hacking campaign where each secured node reshapes the battlefield and pushes the player toward a decisive extraction.
