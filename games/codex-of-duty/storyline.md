# Black Relay

### Premise

The city of Vanta has gone dark behind a hostile signal veil. You are Rook, the last field operative linked to an experimental command network, sent into three sealed districts to reactivate the Black Relay chain. Each uplink restores a fragment of the city's defense grid, but also exposes your position to the Null Guard occupying the streets. Bring every relay online, survive their counterattack, and reach the extraction gate before the network collapses.

### Design

- **Genre:** AAA-style campaign first-person shooter with objective capture and arena-survival encounters
- **Objective:** Activate all three Black Relays and survive the final extraction.
- **Dimension:** 3D
- **Perspective:** first-person
- **Controls:** W/A/S/D or arrow keys · left touch stick · move; mouse movement · drag the right half of the screen · look and aim; left mouse button · FIRE button · shoot; right mouse button or Q · AIM button · focus aim; Shift · push the touch stick to its rim · sprint; Space · JUMP button · jump; R · RLD button · reload; 1/2 or mouse wheel · weapon buttons · switch between pulse rifle and scattergun; E · ACT button · activate a relay; P/Esc · ❚❚ button · pause and resume; H · ? button · guide; T · ✦ button · story; K · ★ button · high scores; M · ♪ button · mute
- **Art direction:** cinematic low-poly military futurism on a disciplined palette—night sky #07111F, wet concrete #15283B, relay cyan #39E6D0, operative amber #FFB547, Null Guard red #FF405D, interface white #EAF6FF. Concrete uses speckled noise and beveled seams, metal carries directional scratches and edge highlights, energy surfaces use scanlines and bloom halos, and combatants use contrasting emissive armor panels for instant recognition.
- **Orientation:** landscape
- **Reference:** Titanfall 2 campaign missions for combat readability, forward momentum, and authored spectacle

### Core Loop

Read the objective beacon, move through the district, use cover and strafing to isolate enemy roles, fire controlled bursts or close with the scattergun, collect armor and ammunition, then enter the relay zone and hold it while the activation meter fills. Every hit, reload, kill, capture tick, and stage transition answers with synchronized visual, audio, and HUD feedback. Activating a relay transforms the arena lighting and opens the route to the next district.

### Challenge and Progression

The opening district teaches movement, aiming, weapon rhythm, pickups, and relay capture against readable riflemen. Later districts add fast melee rushers, distant snipers, shielded enforcers, crossfire lanes, tighter ammunition pressure, faster enemy reactions, and longer contested captures. The player gains a scattergun after the first relay and a short-lived overdrive reward for kill streaks, expanding tactical choices without adding control complexity. Enemy waves are authored so each role has a clear silhouette, sound cue, and counterplay.

### Level Progression

1. **Rainline Approach** — Eliminate 5 riflemen and activate Relay Alpha. Enemies move slowly, attack from one direction, deal light damage, and the wide capture zone fills in 4 seconds.
2. **Glass Market** — Eliminate 8 mixed riflemen and rushers, then activate Relay Beta. Two attack lanes, faster movement, flank pressure, reduced pickup frequency, and a 6-second capture introduce the scattergun.
3. **Ash Transit** — Eliminate 11 riflemen, rushers, and snipers, then activate Relay Gamma. Longer sightlines, coordinated crossfire, higher projectile speed, environmental pulse hazards, and an 8-second contested capture demand target prioritization.
4. **The Black Core** — Defeat 14 enemies including shielded enforcers and stabilize the linked relay. Enemies attack from three directions, shields require flanking or close-range impact, reinforcements arrive faster, and relay instability periodically darkens the HUD.
5. **Last Light Extraction** — Reach the extraction pad and survive 70 seconds. All enemy roles combine in escalating waves, the safe zone contracts twice, elite units become more aggressive, and the final 15 seconds trigger a maximum-intensity assault before evac.

### Winning, Losing and Replay

The run is won when all three named relays are active, the Black Core is stabilized, and the extraction countdown reaches zero. It ends in defeat when health reaches zero; the player can restart the current district or begin a fresh score run. Score rewards accuracy, quick captures, remaining armor, multi-kills, and completion time. Local best scores and the latest run are retained, encouraging cleaner routes, higher difficulty, and mastery of weapon switching.

### Look and Sound

Rain streaks across deep-blue city canyons while cyan relay beams pierce layered fog and red enemy optics cut through the dark. The weapon occupies the lower frame with recoil, muzzle flash, casing sparks, and a responsive reticle; damage direction, objective distance, capture progress, ammo, health, stage, score, and threat warnings form a compact diegetic HUD. Transitions use short mission cards, environmental light sweeps, and restrained camera shake. Generated sound layers distinct rifle and scattergun reports, impact materials, enemy role cues, footsteps, reload mechanics, pickup chimes, relay pulses, UI ticks, low combat percussion, and an adaptive extraction alarm, with every category respecting mute and volume settings.
