# Concepts — Codex of Duty

## 1. Black Relay

An AAA-style 3D first-person campaign viewed through the operative's eyes, faithful to Codex of Duty's current combat-and-relay identity. Keyboard and mouse or twin-stick touch controls drive fast firefights through a rain-lashed city, with crisp military-industrial forms, cinematic lighting, holographic objectives, and battlefield states that visibly change as relays come online.

**Mechanics:** breach three escalating combat districts; fight riflemen, rushers, snipers, and shield units with distinct counterplay; capture relay zones while enemies contest them; switch between pulse rifle and close-range scattergun; trigger an extraction holdout after the final relay

## 2. Ghost Frequency

An AAA-style 3D first-person infiltration game where observation and route planning matter more than clearing every enemy. Keyboard and mouse or twin-stick touch controls let the player scan patrol networks, slip between pools of light, sabotage relay hardware, and escape through a high-contrast city of wet concrete, red security lasers, and cyan signal trails.

**Mechanics:** tag enemies and cameras through a tactical visor; crouch through shadow zones to reduce detection; reroute power to open alternate paths; perform timed relay overrides without breaking cover; escape an alerted district before the lockdown timer expires

## 3. Last Signal

An AAA-style 3D first-person tactical defense game in which the player escorts a fragile autonomous relay drone rather than capturing fixed points alone. Keyboard and mouse or twin-stick touch controls combine mobile gunplay with quick placement decisions, presented through bold near-future architecture, readable enemy silhouettes, luminous defense projections, and escalating siege spectacle.

**Mechanics:** escort the relay drone between uplink sites; place limited barricades and shock turrets; salvage energy from defeated enemies; repair or overcharge the drone during attack waves; choose one of two battlefield upgrades between districts
